Our vision

When a parent asks for last term's slides, when a family joins in January, or when a colleague runs the same session again next year — the answer should be a single link.

Parent training at AISA has always been generous with its content and fragile with its storage. Decks live on laptops. Handouts live in inboxes. Recordings live nowhere at all. This hub is the fix: every resource we build to support families is published here, in full, and stays here — so the work outlives the term it was made for.

One permanent home

Every presentation, handout and guide in one place, organised the way parents actually look for things — not the way our folders are named.

Download, don't request

Slides and documents are downloadable in full. Nothing is view-only, nothing expires, and nobody has to email us to get a copy.

Shareable to the resource

Every collection and resource has its own address. Send a parent straight to the thing they need — not to a homepage and a hunt.

Open to every family

Readable on a phone, works on a slow connection, and built to meet accessibility standards — so no family is locked out of the training.

How to use it

Three steps, no account

Find it

Search by topic or browse the collections. Everything is grouped by what parents ask about, not by which department made it.

Open or download it

Read presentations in the browser, or download the original file to keep, print, or translate. No viewer, no sign-in.

Share the exact link

Use Copy link on any collection or resource and paste it into an email or message. It opens right on that item.

Keep it growing

Adding to the hub

For AISA staff

Publish a resource

Ran a parent session? Add the deck and handouts here so the next person doesn't rebuild them. The contributor guide covers where files go, how to name them and how to add a card to this page.

For parents

Looking for something that isn't here?

The library is being filled collection by collection. If there's a topic you'd like covered, or a session you attended and want the slides for, speak to your child's teacher or the school office and we'll publish it here.
