Why we made this

Couldn't make the session? Lost the handout? Joined us halfway through the year? None of that should mean missing out.

Everything we share with families at AISA lives here, permanently. If you came to a workshop and want the slides, they're here. If you couldn't come, nothing is lost. And if you'd rather read it on your phone after the children are asleep than sit through an evening session, that works too.

Everything in one place

No more searching your inbox for an attachment from last term. Every guide, handout and presentation is here, grouped by topic.

Yours to keep

Download anything you like and keep it. Print it, save it to your phone, or run it through a translator — it's yours.

Easy to pass on

Every topic has its own link. Found something useful? Send it straight to another parent in a single message.

Open to every family

No login, no password, nothing to install. It works on any phone and any connection, at any hour of the day.

The Lion Way

The same values, at school and at home

Your child hears the Lion Way every day at AISA. The resources here are built around those same four values, so what you talk about at home matches what happens in the classroom.

  • Be Responsible
  • Be Safe
  • Be Respectful
  • Be Kind

How it works

Three steps, no account

Find it

Search for a topic or browse the list above. Everything is grouped by the questions families ask us most.

Open it or keep it

Read it here, or download the file to print, save or translate. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to install.

Pass it on

Tap Copy link on any topic and paste it into a message. It opens right on the thing you wanted to share.

Ask us

Can't find what you need?

The library is growing topic by topic. If there's something you'd like covered — or you came to a session and want the slides — speak to your child's teacher or the school office and we'll add it here.

Just joined us

New to AISA?

Start with the New to AISA topic. It gathers everything a family needs in their first weeks, so you're not piecing it together from separate emails.
