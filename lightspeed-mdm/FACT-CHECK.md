## Must be confirmed before publishing

Each row matches a flagged phrase on the page. **Answer with what the Lightspeed
configuration actually does, not what it is capable of doing.**

### Scope and timing

1. **What exactly is "school hours"?** A clock window (e.g. 07:30–15:30), the
   timetable, or something Lightspeed derives itself? Parents will ask about
   after-school activities and exam weeks.
2. **How does the device know it is "on the AISA network"?** SSID, VPN, IP range?
   What happens on a school trip, or on the guest network?
3. **Does anything at all phone home outside those windows** — heartbeat,
   inventory, compliance check? If yes, the page must say so. Saying "nothing is
   logged" is only safe if it is literally true.
4. **Does the profile genuinely stop applying rules outside the window**, or does
   it merely stop *blocking* while still *recording*?

### What the school can see

5. **Web history:** is browsing logged during managed hours only, or retained
   more broadly? Is it identity-linked to the student?
6. **Screen viewing:** can teachers view a student's live screen (e.g. via
   Lightspeed Classroom)? If yes this must be on the page — parents discovering
   it later would be far worse than being told now.
7. **Installed-app inventory:** can the school see the full list of apps on a
   personal device, including the family's own?
8. **Personal files, photos, messages:** confirm the school cannot access these.
   *Do not let this line stay on the page unverified.*
9. **Camera and microphone:** confirm no remote activation.
10. **Location:** confirm no location tracking. If "lost device" mode exists,
    say under what circumstances it can be used and who authorises it.
11. **Remote wipe:** can the school wipe a personal device, in whole or in part?
    If a partial "school data only" wipe exists, describe it plainly.

### Practical

12. **Which platforms** are supported and in scope — iPad, Mac, Windows,
    Chromebook, Android? Does the process differ per platform?
13. **Which year groups or divisions**, and on what dates? The page has
    placeholders for this.
14. **Who does the enrolment** — IT at school, or parents at home following
    instructions? The page currently says IT does it at school.
15. **Removal:** what is the actual process when a child leaves, and can a parent
    request removal mid-year (selling the device, switching schools)?
16. **Opt-out:** is there one? The page currently says "speak to us" rather than
    claiming a right either way. Confirm the real position — this is the line
    most likely to be quoted back at the school.
17. **Performance and storage:** confirm the profile does not meaningfully affect
    either.
18. **Shared family devices:** what is the actual recommendation? The page
    suggests a separate user account.
19. **Contact point:** the name, email and hours for the IT team. Three
    placeholders on the page need this.

### Data and compliance

20. **Where is the data stored**, for how long, and who can access it?
21. **Which regulations apply** — ADEK requirements, UAE data protection, and
    what Lightspeed commits to contractually. Do not cite FERPA or COPPA: those
    are US laws and quoting them in Abu Dhabi will read as copy-paste.
22. **Is there a written privacy notice** for families? If so, link it from the
    page.

## Presenting it

The page doubles as the deck — press **Present this**, or add `#present` to the
URL. Arrow keys or space to move, `F` for fullscreen, `Esc` to exit, and the
notes icon in the control bar hides the speaker notes.

Speaker notes are written into each slide and appear only in present mode. They
are visible on the presenter's screen, so if you mirror your display to a
projector the room will see them — hide them with the notes button before you
start, or present from a laptop using extended display.

Two notes for whoever presents:

- **Slide 5 (the grid) is the one that matters.** Most parental worry comes from
  assuming always-on monitoring of a device the family paid for. Walk all four
  cells slowly.
- **Never expand a claim from the stage.** If a parent asks something not on this
  sheet, take it and follow up in writing. An off-the-cuff reassurance that turns
  out to be wrong is the single worst outcome of this session.
